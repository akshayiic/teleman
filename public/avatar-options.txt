Billed in 6-second steps, 30-second minimum per conversation. Silent time while
connected is billed too, which is why TavusLive.tsx disconnects after 30 s of silence.

2. STRENGTHS AND LIMITATIONS
----------------------------

[1] TAVUS - Anna, Daniel
  Strengths
   + Most realistic: real human video, natural head/eye/expression movement
   + Speaks within ~1 s, any text length
   + Nothing runs locally; works on any device
   + Commercial-ready with SLA; many stock faces, custom face cloning available
  Limitations
   - Highest running cost (1 h/day at ~$0.31/min = ~$560/month)
   - Concurrency limits; one Daily call at a time in the browser
   - Few seconds to connect on first Speak
   - Depends on Tavus servers and pricing (changed several times in 2026)
   - Voice quality and languages depend on Tavus's TTS

[2] LOCAL - Priya, Kavya (LiteAvatar + Piper)
  Strengths
   + Free and unlimited, fully offline and private
   + Faces trained from video: mouth shape follows each sound
   + English and Hindi (Devanagari text auto-detected as Hindi)
  Limitations
   - Only the downloaded gallery faces; no custom face without training
   - CPU-heavy: ~12-14 fps sustained, long texts take up to ~2x spoken length
   - Only the face moves; head and body stay still
   - Heavy setup: ~3 GB vendor folder + Python 3.10 environment
   - One face in memory at a time (~1 GB), ~4 s to switch
   - One user per machine; not built to handle many users
   - Piper voices sound more robotic than Tavus or Kokoro

[3] YOUR PHOTO - Priti (LivePortrait + Piper)
  Strengths
   + Any front-facing photo becomes a presenter; free and offline
   + Light at speaking time (cross-fades pre-rendered mouth images)
  Limitations
   - Least realistic photo-real option: 7 loudness-driven mouth levels,
     mouth shapes don't match individual sounds, face and head barely move
   - ~2 min to prepare each new photo (cached afterwards)
   - Needs a front-facing photo with the mouth visible
   - LivePortrait is MIT, but some original releases depend on non-commercial
     InsightFace models; check the files actually used

[4] PRITI HD (AVTR-1 on GPU)
  Strengths
   + Photo animated with head movement and expressions; close to Tavus quality
   + No per-minute fee, only GPU cost
  Limitations
   - NON-COMMERCIAL licence: cannot go into a product
   - Needs Linux + NVIDIA RTX 20-series or newer (real time from ~RTX 3060 Ti)
   - Renderer, bridge and tunnel must all be up; any one can fail
   - Few seconds of warm-up; buffers when rendering is below real time

[5] 3D AVATARS - Michael, Arjun, Kavita (TalkingHead + Kokoro)
  Strengths
   + Instant start, no cost per use, runs in the browser; scales to any number of users
   + Accurate lip-sync from Kokoro's timing for each mouth shape
   + Good-sounding Kokoro voices (US, UK and Hindi-accent voices)
   + Idle motion, eye contact, blinking, gestures, moods
  Limitations
   - Clearly 3D/CG, not real video
   - Current avatar files are non-commercial (~$800/month or your own model)
   - Needs WebGPU for good speed (slower WASM fallback); first load downloads
     the voice model (~100-300 MB)
   - English text only (Hindi-accent voices read English; no Devanagari)

[6] AUDIO2FACE - Lito, Stan Lee (inactive)
  Strengths
   + High-quality facial animation from any audio, full ARKit blend shapes + emotion
  Limitations
   - NVIDIA's hosted API is deprecated; free trial credits run out
   - Self-hosting needs NVIDIA GPU + AI Enterprise licence (no published price found)
   - Lito is an unrigged scan (jaw faked in a shader); a Stan Lee likeness
     raises likeness/publicity-rights issues for public use
   - Maximum audio length per request; corporate TLS certificate issue on this network


3. WHICH TO USE WHEN
--------------------
 Most realistic, commercial, budget available  -> Tavus (Growth works out to ~$0.31/min)
 Free, private, real-looking, English/Hindi    -> Priya / Kavya (local)
 Custom face, free                             -> Priti; Priti HD for demos/internal only
 Many users at once, zero cost per use, instant -> 3D Michael / Arjun / Kavita, after replacing the
                                                 avatars with commercially licensed ones
